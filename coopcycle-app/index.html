This is a proof of concept

It is not the production CoopCycle app and it is not distributed through the Play Store. It is meant for testing and feedback only: use a test server and test data, never real deliveries or real customer information. Expect rough edges, and expect the app to change or stop working without notice.

A look at the app

What it does

  • Tours and tasks

    Pick a day, see your tours and the pickups and drop-offs in order, and work through them one by one.

  • Map

    Every task on a MapLibre map, with your own position, so you can see what is nearby before you set off.

  • Proof of delivery

    Take a photo or collect a signature when you hand a parcel over, add a note, and upload it with progress you can actually watch.

  • Incidents

    Report what went wrong — nobody home, damaged parcel, wrong address — with a photo and a note.

  • Search

    Pull down on the list to search across addresses, names and order references.

  • Live updates

    Tasks reassigned or added while you ride show up on their own, no pull-to-refresh needed.

  • Over-the-air updates

    New versions of the app arrive by themselves — you only reinstall the APK when the native side changes.

Installing it

Android blocks apps that do not come from the Play Store until you allow it, so there are a couple of extra taps the first time.

  1. Download the APK

    Tap the button at the top of this page on your Android phone. Your browser will warn you that this kind of file can harm your device — that warning appears for every APK; keep the file.

  2. Open the downloaded file

    From the download notification, or from Files → Downloads.

  3. Allow installs from your browser

    Android will offer to take you to Settings → Install unknown apps. Turn on Allow from this source for the browser you downloaded with, then go back.

  4. Tap Install

    If Play Protect asks, choose Install anyway. The app appears as CoopCycle Courier in your app drawer.

  5. Grant the permissions it asks for

    Location (to show you on the map and report your position while working), camera (to take delivery photos) and photos (to attach an existing picture as proof). Nothing else is collected.

Done testing? Uninstall it like any other app: long-press the icon → Uninstall.
